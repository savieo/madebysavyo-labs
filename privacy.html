Privacy & file handling

This page explains how the current MadeBySavyo Labs website handles quote requests, customer-submitted files, reviews and basic website analytics. This is a practical notice for the starter website, not legal advice.

Quote requests and uploaded files

When you submit a quote request, the information you enter and any attached files are processed by FormSubmit and forwarded to savieoseb@gmail.com. The current form supports a combined maximum of 10 MB per submission. Accepted formats are shown beside the upload field.

FormSubmit is an external service. Its own documentation says submissions are retained for 30 days; uploaded files are not available through its submissions API. Please do not upload confidential, regulated, personal or commercially sensitive designs through this starter form. By submitting a file, you confirm you have the right to share it for quotation.

What happens after you submit?

We review the file, dimensions, printability, material and licence considerations. A submission is only a quote request, not an accepted order. We do not begin printing until you approve the quote. The current website does not have customer accounts, a private file vault, order tracking or online payment processing.

Reviews and customer photos

Review and photo submissions are sent to the business inbox for manual review. They are not automatically published. We will ask for permission before featuring a customer review or product photo publicly.

Website analytics

Cloudflare Web Analytics is used for site traffic and performance measurement. It uses a JavaScript beacon to collect website metrics. Please refer to Cloudflare's privacy and analytics documentation for details.

Questions or file removal requests

For questions about a quote request or a file you submitted, contact savieoseb@gmail.com and include the submission date and subject where possible.
