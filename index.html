01 / Start here

New to 3D printing?
Here's how it works.

You don't need to know slicers, nozzle sizes or support settings. Tell us what you're trying to make. We'll guide you through the model, material, quote and print.

01 / IDEASend a file or idea

Upload an STL/3MF, share a model link, or describe the part and what it needs to do.

02 / MODEL CHECKWe inspect the file

We review dimensions, wall thickness, details, orientation, printability and usage rights.

03 / MATERIALChoose the right filament

We help choose based on strength, flexibility, heat, outdoor exposure and available stock.

04 / QUOTEApprove before we print

You'll receive the price, estimated print time and any limitations. No surprise production charges.

05 / MAKEPrint, inspect, collect

We print and check the result, then arrange local Edmonton pickup or an agreed delivery option.

Material guide

Choose the right filament for the job.

Not sure what to pick? Start with what the part needs to do. We'll confirm material compatibility, availability and final suitability when reviewing your request.

Material recommendation is a starting point, not a guarantee. Geometry, printer settings, temperature, load and environment all matter.
PLA — detail and everyday prints

Good surface detail and easy to print. A practical choice for display pieces, organisers and many indoor prototypes. It is not the first choice for hot environments or parts under sustained mechanical stress.

Indoor useFine detailEasy printing

Materials shown are educational options, not a promise that every material or colour is currently in stock. We'll confirm what we can safely print on our machine before quoting.
